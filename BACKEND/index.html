    ABOUT GLOBAL STUDY PORTAL

    Smarter College Management

    A unified digital workspace for students, administrators, individuals and educational institutions to manage academics, attendance, communication, progress and everyday productivity.

    Version 1.0 Active Platform
    Founder
    Aditya Chourasia Founder & Developer
    PLATFORM OVERVIEW

    Built to organize the complete academic experience

    Global Study Portal brings essential educational tools into one responsive and secure platform. It helps users manage daily academic work, institutional communication, student progress and role-based responsibilities without switching between multiple disconnected systems.

    CORE CAPABILITIES

    Everything important, connected in one place

    Attendance Management

    Record and review subject-wise attendance data with organized access.

    Timetable Management

    Maintain lectures, faculty, rooms and academic schedules in one place.

    Progress & Study Analysis

    Track focused study time, goals, consistency and weekly performance.

    Notice Management

    Share important institutional announcements with the correct users.

    Role-Based Access

    Different permissions and experiences for Owner, Super Admin, Admin, User and Individual accounts.

    Organization Management

    Support institutions, branches, groups and individual account workflows.
